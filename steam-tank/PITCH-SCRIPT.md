# 2-minute pitch (~270 words). Fill [brackets] with real facts.

**Problem (0:00)** At [school/town], students juggle tests, projects and activities. In our survey of 92 students, 86% said they struggle with time management, and 66% blamed too much homework. Part of the problem is that teachers plan separately, so three big assignments can land on the same Thursday and nobody sees it coming.

**Solution (0:30)** Steady is a free app that fixes both sides. Each student adds work and gets a forecast: light, moderate or heavy days, with a warning when two major assessments collide. A study planner spreads prep across lighter days.

**What is different (0:55)** Students can share an anonymous code, just dates and types, never names or subjects. Combine a class's codes and Steady shows the best days to schedule the next test. No accounts, no servers, works offline.

**Business (1:15)** Free for students. [Cost: $__/yr for a school license or $0 if club-hosted.] Partners: [counselors, teachers, PTSO]. Customers: students first, then departments and schools.

**STEAM (1:35)** Science: survey and workload research. Technology: offline web app and Android. Engineering: a tested scheduling engine. Arts: calm, color-coded design. Math: scoring and optimization.

**Sustainability (1:50)** It replaces paper planners; we count sheets saved.

**Ask (1:55)** [Pilot with one class for 4 weeks.] Thank you.
